Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 24-48 hours in advance to ensure vehicle availability. However, we do accommodate same-day requests based on fleet availability.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations made 24 hours or more before the scheduled pickup receive a full refund. Cancellations within 24 hours may be subject to a cancellation fee.

Do you offer corporate accounts?

Yes, we offer corporate account management with dedicated support, monthly billing, and customized solutions for businesses of all sizes.

Are your chauffeurs professionally trained?

Absolutely. All our chauffeurs undergo extensive training, background checks, and hold professional licenses. They're trained in defensive driving, customer service, and safety protocols.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), corporate accounts, and bank transfers. Cash payments can be arranged upon request.

Do you provide child seats?

Yes, we provide child seats and booster seats upon request at no additional charge. Please specify the age and weight of the child when booking to ensure the appropriate seat is provided.
